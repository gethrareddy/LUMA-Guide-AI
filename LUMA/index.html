🌟 What Makes LUMA Guide AI Different

1

🧠 Decisions Over Detections

Unlike raw systems that recite every object, LUMA analyzes context & trajectory: Camera → Detection → Context Analysis → Risk/Priority → Guidance.

2

🚨 4-Tier Priority Classification

Prevents sensory overload for blind travelers by filtering non-critical objects:

🔴 Critical Alert Approaching car → Immediate Alert
🟠 Warning Stairs ahead → Warning
🟢 Informational Door nearby → Optional Info
⚪ Low Priority Chair far away → No Alert (Silent)
3

📍 Direction + Distance + Object

Communicates WHAT + WHERE + HOW FAR + HOW IMPORTANT (e.g. "Car approaching from your left, approximately 3 meters away.").

4

👁️ Continuous Environmental Awareness

Passive Mode: Monitors silently and speaks only when important hazards arise.
Active Mode: Directly answers conversational queries ("What's around me?", "Is my path clear?").

5

🎙️ Voice-First Interaction

100% hands-free voice commands, audible acoustic earcons, and instant silence controls tailored for visually impaired navigation.

6

📖 Unified Assistance Architecture

Objects + Text + Scene fused by the Context Engine into a single coordinated priority layer.

7

🔄 Adaptive Situational Intelligence

Dynamically adapts to situations: Normal (quiet), Obstacle, Dangerous Obstacle, Building Doorways, and Proactive Sign Reading.
